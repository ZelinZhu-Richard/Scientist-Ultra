# Beyond UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE: Evidence Contract

**INFERENCE — benchmark status.** The evidence contract is defined and the vNext implementation checkpoint has satisfied several narrow architectural targets. No aggregate architectural winner or empirical superiority claim is established.

**INFERENCE — purpose.** This document defines and scores the conditions under which `THIS_REPOSITORY` may make a bounded architectural or empirical claim relative to `UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE`. It is not a license for an unqualified “beyond” claim.

**INFERENCE — identity rule.** `UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE` always denotes the external system at <https://scientist-one.github.io/> and arXiv:2605.26340. `THIS_REPOSITORY` always denotes the local Scientist-One vNext project. Their names, artifacts, and evidence must not be collapsed.

## Verdict rules

**INFERENCE — architectural vocabulary.** Architectural targets use `ARCHITECTURALLY_STRONGER`, `ARCHITECTURALLY_PARITY`, `ARCHITECTURALLY_WEAKER`, or `UNKNOWN`.

**INFERENCE — empirical vocabulary.** Empirical targets use `EMPIRICALLY_STRONGER`, `EMPIRICALLY_PARITY`, `EMPIRICALLY_WEAKER`, or `NOT_YET_ESTABLISHED`.

**INFERENCE — no scalar shortcut.** An architectural advantage does not imply empirical advantage. A weighted average cannot override a hard failure in citation support, protocol integrity, leakage, baseline fairness, method-code alignment, reproduction, or claim provenance.

## Current local evidence boundary

**FACT_FROM_ARTIFACT — exercised path.** `THIS_REPOSITORY` now exercises a complete synthetic path through controlled literature capture, an advisory provider fixture, evidence-checked problem investigation, typed discovery, a frozen local experiment, all-seed statistics and ablation, a clean system rerun, canonical research-state materialization, independent claim verification, Challenger/soundness gates, and paper/venue blocking. Every material phase registers artifacts and records ledger provenance before downstream use. See [`research_os.py`](../../src/scientist_one/research_os.py).

**FACT_FROM_ARTIFACT — deliberate scientific boundary.** The path is labeled synthetic and `NON_EVIDENTIARY`; live literature is `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`, real-world novelty and generalization are unsupported, `GPU_CLOUD` is `UNTESTED`, `LOCAL_MAC` lacks an OS-enforced sandbox, the soundness verdict is `MORE_EXPERIMENTS_REQUIRED`, release is `BLOCKED_SCIENTIFICALLY`, the paper candidate is `BLOCKED`, venue fit is `NOT_READY`, and E4 is absent.

**FACT_FROM_ARTIFACT — superiority authority boundary.** Value-only superiority checking now produces only a non-authoritative `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY` result. Scientific promotion requires a checked receipt derived by resolving exact frozen registry records for the contract, independent scientific-execution eligibility, aggregate result, statistics, and evaluator assessment; validating their roles, schemas, canonical bytes, hashes, ordered lineage, run-spec/output-manifest custody, isolation/network/protocol attestations, and registry closure; and recomputing the material paired comparison. An aggregate eligibility Boolean or serialized pass cannot authorize promotion. The integrated fixture cannot satisfy this scientific execution boundary, so its `superiority_promotion_diagnostic` is explicitly ineligible and excluded from the registry-derived paper bundle's authoritative evidence.

**FACT_FROM_ARTIFACT — complete gate and paper boundary.** The soundness authority binds registry-verified receipts for all 15 declared dimensions, reviews for all 14 Challenger categories, and every finding. The paper bundle is reconstructed from frozen registry state, verifier receipts, metrics, method-code bindings, soundness, and limitations and is re-resolved during verification. For the non-evidentiary fixture, scientific baseline-completeness and statistics controls remain false and the system-fixture claim is not scientific-writer eligible.

**INFERENCE — capability classification.** The conservative classification is `AUTONOMOUS_EXPLORATION_READY`: the system can autonomously execute and audit a bounded exploration workflow while refusing scientific, publication, and release claims that outrun its evidence. It is not yet `REAL_EXPERIMENT_PIPELINE_READY`, `RESEARCH_GRADE`, or `SUBMISSION_PIPELINE_READY`.

## Decision gates for superiority language

**INFERENCE — prohibited global claim.** The unqualified phrase “THIS_REPOSITORY beats UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE” is prohibited because the systems have not been compared under a complete fair empirical protocol and architecture is multidimensional.

**INFERENCE — permitted architectural claim form.** A narrow claim may use “`ARCHITECTURALLY_STRONGER` on target A-XX” only when that row's implementation, integration, regression, and adversarial evidence is complete and upstream absence or limitation is supported by primary sources.

**INFERENCE — permitted empirical claim form.** A narrow claim may use “`EMPIRICALLY_STRONGER` on evaluation E-XX under protocol P” only when the task, data, evaluator, model/compute/tuning budget, selection regime, uncertainty, and clean reproduction are comparable and fully disclosed.

**INFERENCE — machine-authority rule.** Even when those comparability conditions appear satisfied, superiority language requires a checked promotion receipt that survives registry re-resolution. A value-only diagnostic, an aggregate eligibility flag, or the integrated fixture's `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY` artifact cannot authorize or support the claim and must not enter the paper's authoritative evidence set.

**INFERENCE — parity and weakness rule.** If a comparable result is equal or worse, report `EMPIRICALLY_PARITY` or `EMPIRICALLY_WEAKER`; do not substitute an architectural safeguard to avoid the empirical outcome.

**INFERENCE — current conclusion.** The implementation checkpoint supports narrow `ARCHITECTURALLY_STRONGER` verdicts for A-01, A-02, A-04, A-07, A-10, A-11, and A-13, each only in the stated exercised scope. A-03, A-05, A-06, A-08, A-09, A-12, A-14, and A-15 remain `UNKNOWN`. Every empirical target E-01 through E-10 remains `NOT_YET_ESTABLISHED`; therefore empirical superiority over UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE is **NOT ESTABLISHED**.
