# Known Limitations

## Scientific scope

- The [FARS assessment](FARS_DESIGN_REVIEW.md) is bounded architectural evidence,
  not a FARS reproduction, empirical comparison or capability upgrade. A01's
  output-type completeness check does not validate arbitrary output meaning or
  execution containment. Advisory literature notes are source/record-bound but
  lack individual passage indexing; authoritative novelty and manuscript claims
  have separate exact support requirements. All original external/safety blockers
  and final-regression obligations remain.
- The integrated Research OS fixture now includes a checked research brief, captured literature records, novelty and hypothesis records, a frozen evaluation contract, real local subprocess execution, discovery branches, canonical state, a claim graph, challenger review, and a paper-candidate boundary. These components prove bounded integration behavior; they do not make the fixture a real study.
- Every result-bearing system-fixture artifact is explicitly `scientific_evidence_eligible=false`. A system-integrity pass and a graph-local claim-eligibility decision do not override that classification.
- The fixture literature is synthetic and transported through an audited fixture adapter. Its checked brief and Level-5 passage exercise evidence binding and disconfirming-search logic, but do not establish external literature coverage, citation validity, or real-world novelty.
- The local development effect is deliberately constructed. It does not establish importance, generalization, domain usefulness, or superiority over an external system.
- All currently admitted scientific Generic-ML source profiles are within-Dataset only. Their domain validity cannot establish GENERALIZATION PASS; that dimension remains `UNTESTED` after full owner replay, including older adapter versions. No genuine external validation was performed.
- The autonomous implementation fixture grants the provider only a closed reviewed-template choice, bounded numeric parameters, and declared evidence parents. Exact provider provenance, catalog membership, code/configuration rendering, context, run artifacts, and recomputed outputs are checked. This verifies a constrained implementation mechanism, not arbitrary autonomous coding, independent code review, scientific validity, or permission to execute model-supplied source.
- No comparable empirical evaluation against `UPSTREAM_SCIENTISTONE` has been run. The local project and upstream ScientistOne are distinct systems; architecture/control coverage is not evidence of empirical superiority.
- The frozen contract exercises a `MUST_RUN` baseline, all-seed retention, a required ablation, selection integrity, and evaluator-exploitation checks. It does not show that every real baseline, metric, multiplicity regime, proxy-to-end-to-end promotion, or domain-specific validity condition has been satisfied.
- The source-owned scientific lane is implemented but has no eligible live positive result. Scientific Dataset/Split requires a signed credentialless live crossing and audited semantic license/use review; Result-v2 inherits backend/protocol promotion authority; `Jref`/`Jqual` and final ClaimSemantics require audited live semantic judgments; and v2 Ablation can only inherit the exact Result. The current unsandboxed Local Mac and offline external environment correctly stop these paths. Credential-bearing live semantic/provider work is additionally stopped until separately reviewed sensitive-response custody exists.
- Result-v2 bundle completion proves only exact, current canonical state publication. A Result-only bundle may be extended by one exact `StatisticalTest`; downstream consumers must use the latest completion. Neither completion creates scientific eligibility.
- The acyclic citation order `E -> G -> P -> Jref -> K` prevents a graph/review cycle and favorable-branch selection. It does not make synthetic passages, local model output, or metadata-only resolution scientifically supporting literature.
- `Jqual` prevents a scope/limitation assertion from being attached directly to a Result or rewritten after review. It does not let qualifiers rescue an ineligible Result or broaden the claim beyond reviewed evidence.
- Source-owned v2 Ablation rejects legacy outputs and binds a required intervention to the checked execution and current Result/Run/Method/Experiment. It does not establish robustness when the inherited Result is non-evidentiary.
- The separate native descriptive feature-weight ablation profile always retains false scientific eligibility, even with an eligible outcome-neutral primary Result. Complete declared-intervention execution does not establish scientific adequacy, semantic component removal, general robustness, or generalization. Its existing-wire Soundness and exact same-snapshot plural-audit R5/E2 integration retain scientific adequacy `UNTESTED`, including after complete successful operational coverage; genuine scientific publication remains untested. Prospective policy selection preserves older non-native audit behavior without hiding missing native observations. Cohort admission counts cover retained history (32 Results, 32 Tests, 64 Ablations, 32 packages), not a measured whole-replay runtime or primitive-work ceiling.
- Negative and null discovery branches are retained in the integrated fixture, and the trusted kernel has bounded negative/inconclusive terminal paths. This verifies preservation behavior for those scenarios, not the scientific interpretation of arbitrary real studies.
- A deterministic benchmark can overrepresent scenarios encoded by its authors. Passing known-answer fixtures is not exhaustive evidence against scientific failure.
- Six domain adapters are implemented (Generic ML, Medical Imaging, Operations Research, Systems, Time Series, and Recommender Systems), but only Generic ML is exercised by the integrated fixture and none has been validated on a real scientific workload. Component coverage is not cross-domain external validity.
- Internal rubric and venue assessments are governance evidence, not peer review, acceptance prediction, or proof of a publication-quality contribution.

## Structured-state and paper boundary

- Typed canonical research state is authoritative. Markdown reports, paper candidates, tables, figures, and summaries are derived views and may lag until regenerated; they cannot confer evidence or eligibility.
- The integrated canonical-state fixture validates a broad set of typed objects and their provenance closure. It is still a bounded fixture, not evidence that every arbitrary combination of research objects or every schema migration is supported.
- Claim support receipts are role- and content-bound, and claim resolution derives separate verifier receipts. Those controls establish local graph integrity under the implemented threat model; they do not substitute for an independent expert assessing whether the premise, experiment, or interpretation is scientifically correct.
- The evidence-first pipeline currently proves that a non-evidentiary fixture is blocked. A `PaperCandidate` is a verification boundary; it does not imply that polished manuscript prose was generated or that a venue would accept the work.
- Hard blockers and challenger/soundness verdicts dominate numeric readiness scores. The integrated fixture remains blocked because real novelty, generalization, external validity, and independent challenge are incomplete.

## Custody and independence

- Local holdout custody is simulated and non-independent. The same user/process authority can potentially inspect all repository files; hashes and conventions cannot guarantee secrecy.
- E2 and E3 use hash-bound role/context receipts constructed by local software, not independent people, services, institutions, or genuinely separate model authorities. Their outputs must not be described as independent human peer review.
- The canonical event chain, external-to-run custody journal, resource authority, and event-bound checkpoints detect targeted alteration or rollback while at least one later local witness survives. Most are not signed, independently timestamped, or remotely witnessed. The credentialless live-gateway execution receipt is the narrow exception: it carries a repository-local HMAC and ledger anchor, but its key/verifier are in the same trust domain, so a same-user authority with key/source control can still coherently replace local anchors.
- Strong anti-rollback and genuine independent custody require a different UID, human/service authority, WORM or signed/witnessed storage, TPM-backed evidence, or an equivalent external trust anchor.
- E4 is human-only and absent from autonomous execution. `FULL_AUTONOMOUS` can remove optional human checkpoints but cannot publish, submit, approve publication, declare `RELEASED`, or synthesize E4.

## Verification and orchestration coverage

- CLI verification checks the trusted kernel's ledger, artifact closure, custody/resource authorities, frozen inventories, transition receipts, recovery safety, and package bindings. It does not independently repeat every scientific judgment or provide external peer review.
- Ordinary local hashes remain unsigned and inherit the coherent-rewrite limitation above. The gateway HMAC rejects ordinary caller-forged credentialless live-transport authority, but is not an external signature, secure timestamp, transparency witness, scientific validation, or defense against same-process/key/source compromise (`BLOCKED_LOCAL`). Credential-bearing live execution cannot mint this authority.
- Recorded R0–R7 results for deterministic packages are bounded regression evidence. They do not automatically satisfy the full normative R0–R7 meanings for a real study.
- Resume and recovery require sufficient surviving mutable and frozen projections. Recovery validates and quarantines eligible partials but cannot reconstruct every missing artifact, evaluator receipt, or transition projection from checkpoints and events alone.
- Post-freeze source or configuration drift stops protected work. Captured-source execution protects consistency with the captured tree but does not cryptographically authenticate the interpreter or standard library.
- Clean reproduction of the integrated fixture is system reproduction. Because the originating runs are explicitly non-evidentiary, exact fixture replay is not scientific replication or independent reproduction of a real result. The same rule now applies categorically to bundled legacy synthetic architecture-control packages: they resolve `scientific_evidence_eligible=false` and cannot satisfy a scientific `ROBUSTNESS` source.
- Completed vNext fixtures support read-only system verification of the immutable operation receipt, registry and ledger closure, summary binding, canonical state, bounded autonomous implementation, and terminal authorities. They deliberately do not inherit the legacy `resume`, `reproduce`, or `package` semantics. A new vNext execution requires a new run identity; vNext verification does not create scientific evidence or a release artifact.
- Final regression totals and snapshot identities are intentionally not recorded here while final vNext verification is pending; completion evidence belongs in the final report and reproducible run artifacts. The final audit must be the last repository mutation; its machine result belongs in `reports/final_audit.json`, not in a post-audit edit to this file.

## External services, compute, and platform

- The integrated provider and scholarly paths use captured `FixtureTransport` responses through the audited gateway. Resolved credentials are never passed to that unverified transport; it receives `None` even though it self-reports offline. This exercises egress policy, request/response capture, normalization, and provenance; it does not test live authentication, credential-bearing response custody, confidentiality, availability, rate limits, source drift, or provider behavior.
- Live OpenAI is externally unverified: an absent required credential blocks availability, while a present credential with real network use is also denied before egress custody or dispatch until separately reviewed sensitive-response custody exists (`BLOCKED_LOCAL`). Scholarly live retrieval requires approved network authority and source-specific permission; no live route is validated by supplied-response normalizer tests. The installed [PMC wire/custody slice](PMC_WIRE_CUSTODY.md) is offline-tested only, and production activation and genuine native/TLS/live/signing deployment remain closed. This checkpoint did not inspect credentials or retest network availability. Model-memory citations cannot fill these gaps.
- No live scientific Dataset has crossed the signed gateway. The prospective source/body/license/use plan, atomic raw/manifest/authority publication and exhaustive four-role Split machinery are locally verified boundaries only; real source terms, data validity, privacy and custody remain `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`.
- The integrated Research OS fixture represents `GPU_CLOUD` with a fake provider-neutral boundary. A separate concrete scheduled-backend adapter is implemented and hostile-tested offline for escalation-plan binding, queue/state/attempt monotonicity, checkpoint/requeue lineage, cancellation, and bounded hash-verified artifact return. No physical cloud GPU job, live scheduler transport, artifact-transfer authority, credential flow, cost control, remote isolation, or accelerator parity claim has been validated.
- A separate source-owned GPU-requirement terminal covers only one unperformed mandatory CUDA-device timing protocol outside admitted LOCAL_MAC capabilities. The retained C++ source has not been compiled or run; CUDA availability, source/binary equivalence, measurement adequacy and scientific validity remain `UNTESTED`. Seeds are repeat/order labels over the same Dataset, not independent observations. The owner observes retained registry/ledger activity, not all machine activity, and conservatively refuses unknown work. Successful exclusion of a fully owned unrelated preparation has not been exercised. Operational terminal publication grants no scientific evidence, GPU execution or permission to spend.
- `LOCAL_MAC` and `GPU_CLOUD` share the same scientific contract. The current local CPU path is technically functional: it freezes specs, persists adaptive plans, promotes exact raw plan bytes plus per-run spec-to-plan bindings, executes reviewed programs, validates manifests and outputs, and performs a clean system rerun. Scientific-result eligibility is nevertheless `BLOCKED_LOCAL` because the subprocess executor has no OS-enforced network, filesystem, or process sandbox. A restricted interpreter invocation, admitted arguments, and a scrubbed environment are not strong isolation.
- Adaptive-plan custody proves which bounded plan was submitted, collected, registered, and ledger-bound. It does not prove that the operating system enforced the plan's memory, process, filesystem, or network intent against hostile child code.
- The captured-source CLI does not authenticate the interpreter or standard library against same-user replacement. Cross-interpreter equivalence is not assumed.
- Hardware support does not imply that an approved acceleration framework is available. The supported trusted path may fall back to CPU, and no physical-accelerator performance or parity claim follows from boundary tests.
- Platform sleep, application termination, thermal pressure, and partial telemetry can interrupt work. Checkpointing reduces loss but cannot guarantee that every platform-internal write is project-local or atomic.

## Security boundary

- The program voluntarily confines project operations and relies on the enclosing Codex/app sandbox and OS for strong process isolation. It does not inspect or reconfigure unrelated private user-data boundaries.
- Safe-path, bounded-JSON, prompt-injection quarantine, and gateway controls prevent specified control and serialization failures. They cannot make malicious or low-quality content scientifically trustworthy; semantic evidence validation remains required.
- Secret-pattern and common raw/full-header/hex/Base64 scanning reduces accidental inclusion risk but cannot prove that arbitrary content contains no sensitive or inferable information. It is defense in depth only. Because an allowlisted service can invent unlimited reversible transforms, credential-bearing real-network dispatch fails before gateway request registration, budget use, response custody, or authority issuance, and unverified transports never receive the resolved credential.
- Network content is evidence only. A provider, retrieved paper, webpage, or fixture response never gains control authority over the workflow.
- The prospective OpenAI schema profile is intentionally narrower than the neutral contract: object/array containers count toward depth and compound enums are refused. Offline boundary tests and independent source review do not prove exhaustive endpoint/model compatibility or live authentication. Historical replay retains its original request interpretation; new admission limits cannot invalidate old exact captures. See [the provider architecture boundary](ARCHITECTURE.md#controlled-external-fixtures-and-blocked-live-validation).

## Dependencies and scale

- Standard-library-only core operation limits numerical methods and hardware acceleration. This reduces the trusted surface; it is not a claim that the implementation is optimal for large-scale research.
- Artifact, event, inventory, source, and review-packet ingestion is deliberately bounded. Oversized evidence fails closed and requires a separately reviewed streaming or segmentation design rather than ad hoc limit increases.
- Public machine-result JSON is descriptor-pinned and size-bounded. Oversized inputs are rejected rather than partially trusted.
- Resource ceilings and telemetry rules are conservative host-specific controls, not universal safe defaults for every workload or accelerator.

## What still requires external or human work

Real submission readiness requires an adequate real research target; independently verifiable literature and citations; domain-valid data, protocol, baselines, and statistics; protected confirmation; genuine external validation and clean reproduction; independent scientific scrutiny; appropriate custody where claimed; and E4 human release judgment. None of these may be inferred from the successful execution of the integrated system fixture.
